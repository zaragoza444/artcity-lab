Normal | 1. Executive Summary
Normal | This document is the master technical and development specification for the Art City AI Complete Production Hub. It expands the previously defined Pre-Production Suite into one unified filmmaking and media-production environment spanning development, pre-production, production, post-production, mastering, quality control and delivery. The existing Art City AI Cinema, Studio and Edit products remain first-class parts of the same ecosystem. All modules must share canonical project, scene, shot, take, asset, version, permission and audit data so that a project can move from first idea to approved delivery master without rebuilding its data in separate applications.
Normal | The four visible functions to add are:
Normal | 1. AC Script — AI script writing and professional screenplay editor.
Normal | 2. AC Storyboard — AI storyboard generation plus cinematographic découpage/shot planning.
Normal | 3. AC Budget — production breakdown and deterministic budget calculator/spreadsheet.
Normal | 4. AC Call — shooting call sheet generation, distribution, confirmation and revision control.
Normal | A shared Scene Breakdown + Production Schedule Core must sit underneath the four modules. It may initially remain an internal service, but it is required for accurate budgets, schedules and call sheets.
Normal | 2. Product Positioning and Ecosystem Integration
Normal | The target architecture positions Art City AI as a continuous filmmaking and production pipeline from idea development through pre-production, physical/virtual production, post-production, mastering, quality control and delivery. Cinema, Studio, Edit and the new Production Hub must operate as one ecosystem rather than disconnected products.

TABLE | Search | Implement normalized search aliases for common Arabic/Kurdish letter variants, but never overwrite original names. | Search finds expected Kurdish names even with common ي/ی or ك/ک variant input.
